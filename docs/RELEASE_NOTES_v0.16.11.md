# 工位岛 v0.16.11 / Workday Island v0.16.11

本版本新增“摸鱼农场”，并完善英语学习、股市、云笔记分享和鱼获品质呈现。

This release adds **Slack Farm** and improves English Learning, stock quotes, Cloud Notes sharing, and fishing-rarity presentation.

## 中文

### 摸鱼农场

- 摸鱼岛新增“摸鱼农场”标签页：选择种子、种下蔬菜、等待成长、收获农场币，再逐步开垦新的土地。
- 萝卜可以免费种植；番茄、玉米与南瓜提供更高的收获收益和成长挑战。
- 土地、种植时间、农场币和总收获量仅保存在本机，不上传服务器。

### 六项体验优化

- 英语学习、教材课文和英文书籍阅读时，顶部 `EN` 入口都会正确显示为选中状态。
- 教材原文词汇卡在空间不足时整体换行，完整显示单词、音标和释义，鼠标悬浮也可查看内容。
- 股市行情保留 ETF 等证券需要的三位小数精度。
- 云笔记分享弹窗放大字体、表单、选项和操作按钮，并修复确认按钮展示异常。
- 钓到的鱼按常见、珍稀、史诗、传说展示青绿、蓝色、紫色和金色品质标签。

## English

### Slack Farm

- Added a **Slack Farm** tab in Slack Island: choose seeds, plant vegetables, wait for growth, harvest farm coins, and unlock more plots.
- Radishes are free to plant, while tomatoes, corn, and pumpkins offer higher rewards and longer growth challenges.
- Plots, planting times, farm coins, and total harvests are stored locally only.

### Six experience improvements

- The `EN` header shortcut now remains selected in English Learning, Textbooks, and English Books.
- Textbook vocabulary wraps as whole chips when needed, preserving words, phonetics, and definitions; hover still reveals the full value.
- Stock quotes preserve three decimal places for ETFs and other applicable securities.
- Cloud Notes sharing has larger, clearer typography, forms, options, and actions, and its malformed confirmation button is fixed.
- Fishing catches now show green, blue, purple, and gold labels for common, rare, epic, and legendary rarities.
