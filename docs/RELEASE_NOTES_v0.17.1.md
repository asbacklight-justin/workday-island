# 工位岛 v0.17.1 / Workday Island v0.17.1

本版本完善钓鱼小岛的账号隔离与访客浏览体验，并优化账号中心、浅色主题和 AI 模型选择。

This release improves Fishing Island account isolation and guest browsing, plus Account Centre, light-theme, and AI model-selection polish.

## 中文

- 鱼获背包、永久图鉴、鱼竿、等级、经验和摸鱼币按账号严格隔离；切换账号后不会再显示前一账号资产。
- 未登录用户可先进入钓鱼小岛查看内容；尝试钓鱼或操作需要账号的功能时，才会跳转登录。
- 账号中心改为 3×3 服务布局，并加入「钓鱼小岛」快捷入口。
- 提升浅色主题与浅色会员主题的文字对比度，等级/经验/摸鱼币及侧栏说明更清晰。
- AI 模型选择框完整展示 DeepSeek V4 Flash / DeepSeek V4 Pro。

## English

- Fishing inventory, permanent discoveries, rods, level, XP, and slack coins are strictly isolated by account, preventing assets from another account appearing after a switch.
- Signed-out users can preview Fishing Island first; fishing and account-bound actions ask them to sign in only when needed.
- Account Centre now uses a 3×3 service grid and includes a Fishing Island shortcut.
- Improved text contrast in light and light member themes, including level, XP, slack coins, and side-panel guidance.
- The AI model selector now fully displays DeepSeek V4 Flash and DeepSeek V4 Pro.
